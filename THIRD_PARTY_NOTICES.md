# Third-party material

- Landsat-derived RGB.byte.tif: Rasterio test fixture; included source README records CC0 1.0. Original TIFF and source hashes/provenance are retained under landsat_pilot/data and results/protocol_and_provenance.json. It is an RGB product, not calibrated raw observations.
- astronaut.png: NASA photograph of Eileen Collins distributed with scikit-image v0.25.2; official data documentation identifies public domain/no known restrictions. Source credit and exact URL/SHA256 are in data/new_templates/SOURCE_AND_RIGHTS.md and provenance.json. The subject's appearance does not imply endorsement. No synthetic photograph is used.
- sn-jnl.cls and sn-mathphys-num.bst: Springer Nature author template; retain original headers and notices. Redistribution follows the terms stated in those files; no author-code license replaces them.
- NumPy, SciPy, matplotlib, PyWavelets, pypdf, mpmath and Pillow are external dependencies installed separately with their own licenses. They are not vendored here.
- Vannucci–Corradi and other research publications are cited bibliographically; their PDFs are not included in this release.
